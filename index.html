The Heat

It starts with fire. We don't just brew; we roast the tea leaves and sugar aggressively to caramelize and extract intense, smoky flavors before a drop of water touches them.

water_drop

The Pull (Tarik)

Aeration is key. The "tarik" process cools the intensely hot liquid while creating a thick, frothy head. It's a violent pour back and forth that locks in the signature street-tea texture and tempers the smokiness.
